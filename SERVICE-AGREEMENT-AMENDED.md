## 1. Services Provided

Assurgit agrees to provide the following services to the Client:

**1.1 Website Design**
Creation of UI/UX for the Client's marketing website, including layout, typography, color system, and imagery selection, tailored to the Client's brand.

**1.2 Website Development**
Full front-end development of the marketing website, including approximately eighty-five (85) SEO-optimized service and location pages generated against the project's keyword tier plan (`site-plan.json`, dated 2026-04-29), a homepage, an About page, a Contact page, and a services index/hub.

**1.3 On-Page SEO**
Title and meta tags, structured data (LocalBusiness and Service schema), sitemap, robots.txt, and internal linking implemented per the site plan referenced in Section 1.2.

**1.4 Hosting & Deployment**
Deployment of the finished website to Assurgit's Cloudflare Workers infrastructure, including SSL provisioning and DNS configuration guidance. The Client will receive a working production URL upon launch.

**1.5 Post-Project Support**
Assurgit will provide thirty (30) days of complimentary post-launch support, limited to bug fixes for defects in the work delivered under Section 1. Support does not include new features, design changes, or content additions. Extended support is available under a separate Maintenance Plan.

**1.6 Cybersecurity & Compliance Consulting**
Upon request and under a separate written scope, Assurgit can engage third-party professionals to perform a security or accessibility (WCAG) review. This is not included in the base fee.

## 2. Scope Boundaries — Services NOT Included

The following are **explicitly out of scope** under this Agreement. Any request falling within this section requires a separate written Change Order and additional fees, as detailed in Section 3.4:

- **User accounts, logins, authentication, password resets, member portals, or customer dashboards** of any kind.
- **Online booking, scheduling, or appointment systems.** Assurgit may link to a third-party booking tool the Client already uses (e.g. Vagaro, Square, GlossGenius); deeper integration is a Change Order.
- **E-commerce, payment processing, gift card sales, or shopping cart functionality.**
- **Customer reviews, comments, forums, or any user-generated content features** hosted on the website.
- **Email marketing, SMS, or newsletter platforms.** A simple sign-up form linking to a third-party tool is in scope; integration, template design, and list management are not.
- **Custom web applications, admin panels, or content-management-system builds.**
- **Mobile apps** (iOS, Android, React Native, or Progressive Web App functionality beyond standard responsive web).
- **Photography, videography, original illustration, or video production.**
- **Long-form copywriting beyond the SEO page generation included in Section 1.2** — including hand-crafted blog posts, brand storytelling, or editorial content.
- **Logo design, brand identity work, or print collateral.**
- **Google Business Profile setup, optimization, posting, or management.** GBP services are governed by the separate Addendum A.
- **Ongoing SEO services, link building, or content marketing** beyond the initial launch.
- **Ongoing maintenance, monitoring, security patching, or content updates** after the support window in Section 1.5.
- **Migrations from or integrations with** existing third-party platforms (Squarespace, Wix, Mailchimp, POS systems, CRM software).
- **Translation or multi-language support.**
- **Custom analytics dashboards or BI reporting.** A standard Google Analytics (GA4) install is in scope; custom dashboards are not.
- **Training sessions, recorded tutorials, or written documentation** beyond a brief launch handoff.

If a service is not listed in Section 1, it is not included.

## 3. Project Timeline, Revisions, and Payment Terms

> **Section 3 reflects the payment-term modification requested by the Client on 29 April 2026 and supersedes Section 3 of the Original Agreement in full.**

**3.1 Initial Wireframe / Mockup**
Following the initial consultation, Assurgit will provide a wireframe or mockup for the Client's review. Upon approval, the Client must pay the Deposit set out in Section 3.5 before development begins.

**3.2 Revisions During Design Phase**
The Client is entitled to two (2) rounds of revisions during the design phase, covering copy edits, image swaps, and layout adjustments within the existing design system. Revisions that require redesign of the underlying template or color system are a Change Order.

**3.3 Revisions During Development Phase**
Once development begins, the Client is entitled to one (1) revision request per week at no additional cost. Additional revision requests beyond this allowance will be billed at $70 per hour or as a flat fee agreed in advance.

**3.4 Out-of-Scope Requests and Change Orders**
Any work falling outside Section 1 — including all items listed in Section 2 — will be handled as follows:

(a) The Client submits the request in writing (email is sufficient).
(b) Assurgit responds with a written Change Order specifying the scope, fixed fee or hourly estimate, and timeline impact.
(c) **No out-of-scope work begins until the Change Order is signed by the Client and any required deposit is paid.**
(d) Verbal requests, requests made in passing, or "while you're in there" asks are not authorization. Assurgit will redirect such requests to this process.

**3.5 Total Project Fee and Revised Payment Schedule**

The total fixed fee for the services in Section 1 is **$__________** (the "Project Fee").

At the Client's request and as the principal modification effected by this Amended Agreement, the Project Fee shall be paid as follows:

(a) **Deposit — $50.00**, paid upon execution of this Amended Agreement and prior to commencement of any further work. The Deposit is **non-refundable** and is credited against the Project Fee.

(b) **Balance — Remainder of the Project Fee**, due in full upon completion and delivery of the finished website. "Completion" means the website has been deployed to its production URL and Assurgit has notified the Client in writing that the work under Section 1 is ready for review.

(c) **No intermediate milestone payments** are required under this Amended Agreement. The installment schedule contemplated by the Original Agreement is hereby cancelled.

**3.6 Effect of Non-Payment of Balance**
If the Balance is not paid within fourteen (14) days of the completion notice in Section 3.5(b), the unpaid amount shall accrue interest at one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month. If the Balance remains unpaid for thirty (30) days after the completion notice, Assurgit may suspend hosting and remove the deployed site from its infrastructure until payment is received in full. The Client's content ownership rights under Section 6.1 vest only upon receipt of full payment.

**3.7 Project Milestones**
Notwithstanding the simplified payment schedule, Assurgit will continue to outline clear milestones for design, development, and launch and will provide regular progress updates to the Client. Achievement of an internal milestone is not a payment trigger under this Amended Agreement.

# ADDENDUM A — GOOGLE BUSINESS PROFILE OPTIMIZATION

This Addendum is **separate from and additional to** the Service Agreement above. Google Business Profile ("GBP") services are a distinct engagement and are **not included** in the website fee under Section 1. Execution of the Service Agreement does not constitute purchase of the services in this Addendum, and vice versa.

## A.1 Scope of GBP Services

Assurgit will perform a one-time GBP optimization, which may include:

- Claiming or verifying the GBP listing, or coordinating verification with the Client.
- Configuring categories, services, and attributes.
- Drafting business description, hours, and service-area settings.
- Initial photo upload using photos provided by the Client.
- NAP (Name / Address / Phone) consistency check between GBP and the website.
- Initial Q&A seeding and review-response template setup.
- Linking GBP to the website and embedding location signals (schema, embedded map).

## A.2 What Is NOT Included in This Addendum

- Ongoing GBP posting, weekly updates, or content-calendar management.
- Review generation, review-solicitation campaigns, or paid review tools.
- Responding to customer reviews on the Client's behalf, beyond providing templates.
- Local citation building (Yelp, BBB, industry directories) beyond the GBP listing itself.
- Paid Google Ads, Local Service Ads, or any paid placement.
- Disputes, suspensions, or reinstatement work — billed hourly if required.

## A.3 Fees

- **One-Time GBP Optimization Fee:** $80
- **Optional Monthly GBP Management:** $70 per month, under a separate written agreement.

## A.4 Dependencies and Disclaimers

- The Client must grant Assurgit Manager-level access to the GBP listing.
- If verification requires a postcard or phone call to the business address, the Client is responsible for completing that step.
- Search and local-pack ranking outcomes depend on factors outside Assurgit's control. No specific ranking, call volume, or visibility outcome is guaranteed.
