## 1. Services Provided

Assurgit agrees to provide the following services to the Client:

**1.1 Website Design**
Creation of UI/UX for the Client's marketing website, including layout, typography, color system, and imagery selection and tailored to the Client's brand.

**1.2 Website Development**
Full front-end development of the marketing website, including approximately eighty-five (85) SEO-optimized service and location pages generated against the project's keyword tier plan (`site-plan.json`, dated 2026-04-29), a homepage, an About page, a Contact page, and a services index/hub.

**1.3 On-Page SEO**

**1.4 Hosting & Deployment**
Deployment of the finished website to Assurgit's Cloudflare Workers infrastructure, including SSL provisioning and DNS configuration guidance. The Client will receive a working production URL upon launch.

**1.5 Post-Project Support**
Assurgit will provide thirty (30) days of complimentary post-launch support, limited to bug fixes for defects in the work delivered under Section 1. Support does not include new features, design changes, or content additions. Extended support is available under a separate Maintenance Plan.

Upon request and under a separate written scope, Assurgit can engage third-party professionals to perform a security or accessibility (WCAG) review. This is not included in the base fee.

## 2. Scope Boundaries — Services NOT Included

The following are **explicitly out of scope** under this Agreement. Any request falling within this section requires a separate written Change Order and additional fees, as detailed in Section 3.4:

- **User accounts, logins, authentication, password resets, member portals, or customer dashboards** of any kind.
- **Online booking, scheduling, or appointment systems.** Assurgit may link to a third-party booking tool the Client already uses (e.g. Vagaro, Square, GlossGenius); deeper integration is a Change Order.
- **E-commerce, payment processing, gift card sales, or shopping cart functionality.**
- **Customer reviews, comments, forums, or any user-generated content features** hosted on the website.
- **Email marketing, SMS, or newsletter platforms.** A simple sign-up form linking to a third-party tool is in scope; integration, template design, and list management are not.
- **Custom web applications, admin panels, or content-management-system builds.**
- **Mobile apps** (iOS, Android, React Native, or Progressive Web App functionality beyond standard responsive web).
- **Photography, videography, original illustration, or video production.**
- **Long-form copywriting beyond the SEO page generation included in Section 1.2** — including hand-crafted blog posts, brand storytelling, or editorial content.
- **Logo design, brand identity work, or print collateral.**
- **Google Business Profile setup, optimization, posting, or management.** GBP services are governed by the separate Addendum A.
- **Ongoing SEO services, link building, or content marketing** beyond the initial launch.
- **Ongoing maintenance, monitoring, security patching, or content updates** after the support window in Section 1.5.
- **Migrations from or integrations with** existing third-party platforms (Squarespace, Wix, Mailchimp, POS systems, CRM software).
- **Translation or multi-language support.**
- **Custom analytics dashboards or BI reporting.** A standard Google Analytics (GA4) install is in scope; custom dashboards are not.
- **Training sessions, recorded tutorials, or written documentation** beyond a brief launch handoff.

If a service is not listed in Section 1, it is not included.

## 3. Project Timeline, Revisions, and Payment Terms

**3.1 Initial Wireframe / Mockup**
Following the initial consultation, Assurgit will provide a wireframe or mockup for the Client's review. Upon approval, the Client must pay the agreed deposit before development begins.

**3.2 Revisions During Design Phase**
The Client is entitled to two (2) rounds of revisions during the design phase, covering copy edits, image swaps, and layout adjustments within the existing design system. Revisions that require redesign of the underlying template or color system are a Change Order.

**3.3 Revisions During Development Phase**
Once development begins, the Client is entitled to one (1) revision request per week at no additional cost. Additional revision requests beyond this allowance will be billed at $__70__ per hour or as a flat fee agreed in advance.

**3.4 Out-of-Scope Requests and Change Orders**
Any work falling outside Section 1 — including all items listed in Section 2 — will be handled as follows:

(a) The Client submits the request in writing (email is sufficient).
(b) Assurgit responds with a written Change Order specifying the scope, fixed fee or hourly estimate, and timeline impact.
(c) **No out-of-scope work begins until the Change Order is signed by the Client and any required deposit is paid.**
(d) Verbal requests, requests made in passing, or "while you're in there" asks are not authorization. Assurgit will redirect such requests to this process.

**3.5 Deposit**
Upon approval of the initial wireframe/mockup, the Client shall pay a non-refundable deposit of $17 before Assurgit proceeds with development.

**3.6 Payment Schedule**
The Client may choose from the following payment structures:

**Option 1 — Full Payment**
The Client pays one hundred percent (100%) of the remaining project balance upon completion of the design phase.

**Option 2 — Installments**
- 25% of the remaining balance upon approval of the design phase
- 25% upon completion of the homepage and page templates
- 25% upon completion of the bulk service-page generation
- 25% upon final delivery and launch

**3.7 Late Payment**
Invoices are due within fourteen (14) days of issuance. Balances unpaid after thirty (30) days accrue interest at 1.5% per month. Assurgit may suspend work and remove the deployed site from its infrastructure if any invoice is more than thirty (30) days overdue.

**3.8 Project Milestones**
Assurgit will outline clear milestones with timelines for design, development, and launch, and will provide regular progress updates to the Client.
